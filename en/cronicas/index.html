02 · A literary project

365 chronicles. Stories at their own pace.

Chronicles about everyday life, strange human logic, the unexpected and that urge to laugh when life insists on contradicting our reports. Written in Portuguese; translations are added as they are reviewed.

The 365 Chronicles series grows as each story is approved, without a fixed publishing quota or deadline. Chronicles remain independent of the monthly magazine issues. See monthly issues.

The book begins in the magazine

A book that grows with each new story.

The chronicles have a life of their own. Some talk to data. Others just need good characters, a problem and a bit of humor.

The project A Realidade Dá Sinais — 365 Crônicas (Reality Sends Signals — 365 Chronicles) began on November 1, 2026. All texts are original work by Gustavo Santos.

The book, in Portuguese, is on digital pre-sale — e-book and audiobook — on Benfeitoria until January 6, 2027.
