The idea · No jargon

Data is about people.

Data-Driven Culture is a way of investigating reality, questioning certainties and making more conscious decisions — inside a company and outside it.

The signal starts with the question

Picture two companies.

The first has dashboards for everything, but nobody may disagree with the director. The second has a slightly crooked spreadsheet, a curious team and the freedom to ask why things did not go as expected.

A data-driven culture starts closer to the second one. Technology helps a lot, but it does not replace curiosity, responsibility and the ability to change your mind.

  1. SignalSomething happens.
  2. QuestionWhy did it happen?
  3. EvidenceWhat can we verify?
  4. DecisionWhat changes because of it?

What does this look like in real life?

In a shop, it might be demand for a product that is not on the shelf yet. In football, a change of pattern during matches. In the environment, a series of measurements that calls for investigation. In everyday life, a statistical surprise.

That is the universe this magazine explores: science and business without losing humor, doubt and the stories of real people.
