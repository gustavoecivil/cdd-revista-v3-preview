Privacy

Privacy policy.

A magazine about data should be clear about what it does with yours. Version of November 8, 2026.

Who is responsible

Cultura Data-Driven magazine is edited by Gustavo Santos. Questions or requests about your data: gustavo@coutosantos.com.

What we collect when you read

The magazine has no sign-up, no login, no forms, and no audience analytics or advertising tools. We do not sell or share reader data.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which logs technical access data (such as IP address and browser) to deliver pages and protect the site against attacks.

Third-party services on the pages

  • Google Fonts: the site fonts are loaded from Google servers, which receive the visitor’s IP address.
  • YouTube: videos use privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube only receives data when you press play.
  • External links: stores, Benfeitoria, Amazon, WhatsApp and social networks have their own policies. Amazon links are affiliate links, as disclosed in the Library.

The Lab game stores your progress only in your browser.

When you contact us

If you write by email or WhatsApp, we use your name and contact details only to reply. The conversation is not used for marketing or passed on to third parties.

Social media publishing

The magazine uses its own tool, authorized by the editor, to publish articles on his LinkedIn profile. It only accesses the editor’s own account and collects no reader data.

Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you may request access to, correction or deletion of your contact data. Just write to gustavo@coutosantos.com.
