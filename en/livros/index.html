Storytelling with Data

Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic’s book is a starting point for studying how to present information clearly. In the magazine we learn by experimenting — without reproducing the book’s pages or turning the experience into school notes.

We start with small editorial decisions: which question do we want to answer, what information is missing, and what headline does the chart allow us to write?
