Editor’s letter · November 2026

The world speaks all the time. Data helps us listen.

We are building a magazine for people who are not satisfied with the first chart and cannot resist a good story. There is room here for business, football, behavior, the environment, science, literature, doubts and even the fruit stand on the corner. You do not need to love math to be curious.

The blog investigates. The chronicles observe life. The books invite practice. The lab puts hypotheses to the test. The videos set numbers in motion.

— Gustavo Santos, editor
